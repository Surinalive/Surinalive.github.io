  • Unnamed Multiplayer Restaurant Management Game

    This is a project that's been on my mind for a bit after reminiscing about older games I used to be obsessed with. Think diner dash series and cake mania. Key game mechanics:

    • (Think diner dash mechanics!) Players claim patrons and place them at tables. Once a player claims someone, they are responsible for serving the patron until they leave.
    • Workers are responsible for cooking, serving, and doing dishes.. (more mechanic such as entertaining guests, smoozing with politicians/investers to expand, maybe starting a garden, etc...)
    • Players can hire people(bots) to do ^^ roles if they don't like the mechanic or only like one aspect of gameplay HOWEVER they must train the workers!!! Workers start off slow and make lots of mistakes but as they gain experience, they become faster until they reach a level of mastery. Player can train the workers by showing them how to do the tasks to increase their experience significantly (maybe do the task 15 times for mastery). The idea to make it not too easy was workers have to be trained for each individual recipe/chore,
    • EVERYTHING can be replaced by workers so players have lots of flexibility and we can meet multiple playstyles!
